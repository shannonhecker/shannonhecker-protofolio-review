## Presentation rules

- All product stills retain their full source proportions, with full-resolution links. No zoom crop, parallax enlargement or stretched aspect ratio.
- Source workspaces were captured at enough height to include complete lower charts. Tables retain the live product's own scrolling behavior.
- Videos use native controls and load only on request. They do not autoplay or loop. The active game cover is a static Kaohsiung illustration; the earlier mixed-city four-second cover video is retained historically but not referenced.
- Fusion's public rebuild includes AG Grid trial watermarks. These remain visible, unaltered. Removing them belongs in that source application after its licensing/configuration is resolved.
- FX is a public rebuild with simulated data. Classic represents the Barclays design; Enhanced is Shannon's rebuild, not a claim of a shipped Barclays release.
- The FX state lane and counted clusters are clearly labelled design refinements because the current live rebuild still shows patterned regions and individual fills.
- Current uoaui export does not force comparison or tuning. The case describes these as Shannon's review practice.
- Old product screenshots and the old uoaui demo are no longer referenced by the three case pages or their index thumbnails. Historical source files may remain in the repository; they are not presented as current evidence.
- Winking Star's supplied public assets remain; its full hero illustration and phone images now retain their own proportions. Current approved Sample Family / Monday / iOS story takes precedence over older Figma drafts.
