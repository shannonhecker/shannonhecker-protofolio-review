# Persona illustration update, 11 October 2026

Shannon requested a matched illustration family, with the FX personas as the visual benchmark, including four new Deal Model roles. All fifteen illustrations were generated with Nano Banana 2 through Creative Claw. The initial Higgsfield submission was rejected before generation because that workspace had no credits.

The source images establish drawing style and, for existing personas, appearance. These are fictional design personas and composite roles, not photographs of research participants. Role, quote, goals and frictions remain the authored content.
